# InvoiceChat: marketing strategy (Meta, TikTok, YouTube + leads)

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** *Professional invoices and quotes in two minutes, from your phone, sent by WhatsApp. Free to start, R50 a month for more.*

**Who we sell to:** every South African business that sends invoices or quotes and finds it a chore:

| Segment | Pain we fix | Hook that lands |
|---|---|---|
| Trades: plumbers, electricians, builders, mechanics | Quote on site, invoice at night in Word | "Send the quote before you leave the client's yard" |
| Beauty, salons, barbers, nails | Look unprofessional, clients pay late | "Look like a big salon on paper" |
| Caterers, events, decor, photographers | Deposits and quotes get lost in WhatsApp chats | "Quote → deposit → invoice in one chat" |
| Freelancers & consultants (design, IT, marketing) | Templates, VAT maths, chasing payment | "Stop fighting Word templates" |
| Spaza shops, resellers, side hustles | No laptop, no card | "Works on any phone; pay with an OTT voucher" |
| Small companies with an admin person | Many invoices, inconsistent branding | "Every invoice on-brand, numbered and tracked" |

**Why us over Excel/Word/Zoho:** no learning curve (it's a chat), works on any phone, WhatsApp delivery, free logo maker, AI drafts from one sentence, rand-priced, and no card needed.

**Pricing story:** Free (5 a month), Pro **R50/month** ("less than a Nando's quarter chicken a month"), top-up **R10 = 10 invoices**. Every free PDF carries "Created free with InvoiceChat", which turns each invoice a user sends into an advert to another business.

## 2. The funnel and numbers to watch

```
Ad / video / call / email  →  Landing page  →  Sign-up  →  First invoice  →  Paying (Pro or top-up)
          CTR 1–2%             sign-up 15–25%       activation 60%+          paid 5–10% in 60 days
```

| KPI | Where to see it | Target, first 90 days |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per sign-up (CPL) | Meta / TikTok Ads Manager (CompleteRegistration) | under R15 |
| Activation (made a document) | Admin Dashboard → "Made a document" | 60%+ |
| Finished profile | Dashboard → "Finished profile" | 70%+ |
| Paid conversion | Payments → revenue, Pro customers | 5–10% of active users |
| Lead calls → sign-ups | Leads & calls → "Signed up from leads" | 5–8% of contacted leads |
| Sources | Dashboard → "Where sign-ups come from" | Know your best channel by week 3 |

Rule of thumb: the business works when **cost per paying customer < 3 months of Pro (R150)**.

## 3. Content engine (organic, all platforms)

Make **one vertical video**, post it everywhere: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts. **Admin → Social & ads → New post** sends it to all four at once (TikTok as a draft to add a sound).

**Cadence:** 1 video a day on weekdays, plus 2 static posts a week. Post at 07:00–08:30, 12:00–13:00 or 18:00–20:00.

**Five content pillars (rotate):**

1. **Speed demo (40%)**: screen-record the phone: "Watch me invoice a client in 47 seconds." Show the PDF arriving on WhatsApp. This is your best ad.
2. **Before / after (20%)**: an ugly Word invoice next to the InvoiceChat one. "Which business would you pay first?"
3. **Money tips (15%)**: "3 things every invoice in SA must have" (VAT number, due date, bank reference); "How to stop clients paying late."
4. **Customer stories (15%)**: a real user (plumber, baker, nail tech) saying how much time they save. Offer users a free month of Pro for a 20-second video.
5. **Founder / behind the scenes (10%)**: why you built it, new features ("We just added AI: type one sentence, get an invoice").

**Hooks (first 2 seconds, on screen):**
- "Still doing invoices in Word at 10pm?"
- "This plumber sends quotes before he leaves the client's house."
- "Your invoice is why they pay late."
- "I made a logo and an invoice in 2 minutes. Free."
- "No laptop? No problem. Invoice from WhatsApp."
- "R50 a month vs. your Sunday afternoons."

**15-second script template:**
> (Hook, 0–2 s) "Still typing invoices in Word?" → (Demo, 2–10 s) phone screen: type "Invoice Acme for 3 logos at R500", PDF appears, tap Send via WhatsApp → (Proof, 10–13 s) "Free for 5 a month, R50 for more" → (CTA, 13–15 s) "Link in bio. Try it on your next client."

## 4. Paid plan by platform

Start with **R3,000–R5,000 for the first month**, then move money to whatever has the lowest cost per *activated* user.

### Meta (Facebook + Instagram): main engine, about 50% of budget
- **Objective:** Leads/Sales → website conversions, optimise for **CompleteRegistration** (the pixel + Conversions API you set up send it).
- **Campaign 1, Prospecting (Advantage+ audience):** South Africa, ages 25–55, R100–R150/day. Let Meta find buyers; don't over-target.
- **Campaign 2, Interest tests (R50/day each, 7 days):** small business owners, self-employed, entrepreneurship, hair salons, construction, catering, freelancing. Kill anything with CPL above R25 after R250 spent.
- **Campaign 3, Retargeting (R30/day):** website visitors in the last 30 days who didn't sign up. Creative: "Your first 5 invoices are free" + customer testimonial.
- **Creatives:** 3–5 at a time: the speed demo video, before/after carousel, testimonial, a "R50 a month" static. Refresh weekly.
- **Link:** `https://invoicechat.swiftfusion.co.za/register.php?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=<name>`
- **Also:** list the service on Facebook Marketplace (Services) and post in local business groups (where allowed) with the speed demo.

### TikTok: reach and cheap video views, about 30% of budget
- **Organic first:** daily videos from the pillars above; reply to comments with video replies (a great source of content).
- **Paid:** Web conversions campaign optimised for **CompleteRegistration**. TikTok's minimum is about $20/day (≈R380) per ad group, so run **one** ad group with 3–4 videos. **Spark Ads** (boosting your best organic videos) usually beat new ads.
- **Creator deals:** pay 3–5 small SA business creators (5k–50k followers; plumbers, salon owners, side-hustle accounts) R500–R1,500 per video to show how they invoice. Ask for usage rights so you can run them as Spark Ads.
- **Link:** `…/register.php?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=<name>`

### YouTube: search intent and trust, about 20% of budget
- **Shorts:** every video above goes to YouTube Shorts automatically (connected channel).
- **Long-form (1 a week, 4–8 min, screen recording + voice):** these rank for searches people already make:
  - "How to make an invoice on your phone (South Africa)"
  - "Free invoice template South Africa: easier way"
  - "How to write a quotation for a client"
  - "VAT invoice requirements South Africa explained"
  - "How to get clients to pay on time"
  Put the sign-up link (`utm_source=youtube`) in the first line of the description and as a pinned comment.
- **Paid (Google Ads → Video, R50–R100/day):** in-feed ads on those how-to searches and a **Demand Gen** campaign using the speed demo. Optimise for sign-ups once GA4 is linked to Google Ads.

## 5. Leads: calls, WhatsApp and email (Admin → Leads & calls)

The lead system is your cheapest channel. Work it daily:

| Day | Action | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Fri morning | **Email campaign** (Cold intro) to fresh business leads | 100–200/day to start, growing to 300/day |
| Next day | Those leads appear on the **Call list**; call with the cold-call script | 30–50 calls/day per caller |
| On the call | "Interested" → **WhatsApp → Send the link** from the card → mark *Interested* | |
| +3 days | Interested leads come back for the **follow-up script** | |
| Weekly | Re-email *unreachable* leads with the Follow-up template | |

**Best lists first:** those with a business column and a mobile (WC Business data, list12, list19, list21, list22 rows with business names, list28, list29, sa1–sa11). Use **Fill today's list → Businesses only**.

**Call-centre basics:** 40 dials ≈ 12 conversations ≈ 4 interested ≈ 1–2 sign-ups. Track it in the Leads KPIs. A part-time caller paid per sign-up (e.g. R20 per activated user) pays for itself after the first top-up.

**Compliance (POPIA / CPA), non-negotiable:**
- Only use lists you have the right to use; they must be business contacts or people who opted in.
- Every email has an unsubscribe link (automatic). Anyone who asks is marked **do not contact**, which is permanent.
- Call business hours only (Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00, Sat 09:00–13:00; no Sundays or public holidays).
- Never store or use ID numbers, bank or credit data from the files. The importer ignores them.

## 6. Retention and upgrades (turn sign-ups into paying users)

- **Day 0:** welcome email (automatic) + the AI "Describe it" button in the chat.
- **Day 1, no document yet:** a call from the leads team: "Can I help you send your first invoice?"
- **When the 5 free invoices run out:** the chat explains top-up (R10) and Pro (R50) with the OTT steps. This is the main upgrade moment.
- **Referrals (next feature to build):** "Give a business 10 free invoices, get 10 free" via a referral link.
- **Every free invoice footer** brings new businesses in. Track sign-ups with no source as "footer/referral".
